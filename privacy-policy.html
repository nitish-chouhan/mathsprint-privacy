MathSprint Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 10, 2026

1. Overview

MathSprint is a mathematics practice application developed by Nitish Chauhan ("we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use the app, how it is used, and your rights regarding that information.

By downloading or using MathSprint, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

MathSprint does not require you to create a user account.

The app stores certain information locally on your device to provide its features, including:

  • Your name (if you choose to enter it during onboarding).
  • Practice session history, including scores, topics practiced, question results, and response times.
  • Practice streaks, daily goals, and weekly plans.
  • App preferences such as theme, timer, and haptic feedback settings.
  • Local counters used to manage advertisement frequency.

This information is stored on your device and is not intentionally transmitted to servers operated by us.

3. Third-Party Services

MathSprint uses Google AdMob (Google LLC) to display advertisements.

Google may collect and process certain information necessary to provide, measure, and improve advertising services. This may include:

  • Device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID).
  • IP address.
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address.
  • App interaction and diagnostic information.

The collection and processing of this information is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Google Privacy Policy

Where required by applicable law, MathSprint will request your consent before serving personalized advertisements.

You may also manage your advertising preferences through your Android device settings.

4. Permissions

MathSprint requests only the permissions necessary for its functionality.

  • Internet – Required to load advertisements and online resources.
  • Vibrate – Used to provide haptic feedback during app interactions.

MathSprint does not intentionally request or use unnecessary permissions such as precise location, microphone, camera, or storage access.

5. Children's Privacy

MathSprint is intended for a general audience.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Information stored by MathSprint remains on your device.

You may remove all locally stored information at any time by:

  • Clearing the application's data through your device settings.
  • Uninstalling the application.

7. Future Changes to the App

As MathSprint evolves, we may introduce new features or services.

If those changes affect how information is collected, used, or stored, this Privacy Policy will be updated before those changes take effect.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

Any changes will be reflected by updating the Last Updated date at the top of this document. Continued use of the app after changes become effective constitutes your acceptance of the revised Privacy Policy.
